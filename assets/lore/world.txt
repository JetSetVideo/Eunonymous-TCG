SYNTHOPWARS - WORLD OF SYNTHOPIA
(Lore & Lexicon — v3.0, reconciled with docs/CARDS.md and GAME_RULES.md)

================================================================
PART I — THE GREAT VAULT
================================================================

In the year 2187, six Neo-Cities discovered the Great Vault - an ancient repository of infinite power
buried beneath the ruins of Old Earth. The Vault is said to contain the source code of reality itself,
and whoever controls it can reshape the world.

Every Commander who marches on the Vault marches as one of nine kinds of thing a person can become
in Synthopia. That choice — Character, not city, not faction — is the first decision a Commander makes,
before they ever draw a card. See PART III.

================================================================
PART II — THE SIX NEO-CITIES
================================================================

1. DALIAN BLOCK (Cyber-rain)
   - A city of perpetual rain and neon lights
   - Information is the true currency
   - Home to the Overchargers and Control Syndicate tribes
   - Traps are hidden in shadows; visibility is low
   - Famous for: Data markets, underground hacking rings
   - Strongest Character presence: Cyborg, Memetic, Schemist

2. MASHHAD MEGACITY (Desert storm)
   - Built in the remnants of ancient deserts
   - Sandstorms are a way of life
   - Home to the Chaos Cult and Titanborn tribes
   - Speed is everything; slow creatures die young
   - Famous for: Arena battles, sand-sailing races
   - Strongest Character presence: Technopriest

3. LONDON REALITY (Fog)
   - Shrouded in eternal fog
   - Deception and espionage are arts
   - Home to the Control Syndicate and Synth Legends tribes
   - Nothing is visible until it's too late
   - Famous for: Shadow wars, illusions, secret societies
   - Strongest Character presence: Ethereal, Schemist

4. STAR BEIJING (Neon overload)
   - The brightest city in the world
   - Energy courses through every street
   - Home to the Overchargers and Economy Lords tribes
   - Power is abundant but dangerous
   - Famous for: Energy duels, neon gardens
   - Strongest Character presence: Synthetic, Organizer

5. PEOPLE'S REPUBLIC OF LAHORE (Golden hour)
   - A sanctuary of healing and restoration
   - The most beautiful city in the Neo-World
   - Home to the Fortress Guild and Synergy Collective tribes
   - Healing is doubled; life flourishes
   - Famous for: Healing springs, botanical gardens
   - Strongest Character presence: Biopure

6. FUTURE ODESSA (Gravity wells)
   - Where reality bends and gravity warps
   - The most mysterious city
   - Home to the Chaos Cult and Economy Lords tribes
   - Space is limited; everything is compressed
   - Famous for: Gravity surfing, dimensional rifts
   - Strongest Character presence: Refinist

================================================================
PART III — THE NINE CHARACTERS
================================================================

A Character is who you play as: it decides which signature cards you can ever hold, and the five-axis
shape (Cost, Stats, Engine, Choice, Tempo) of the fights you're built to win. Mechanical detail — signature
cards, exact axis ratings, deck-construction rules — lives in docs/CARDS.md. This section is the myth
behind the mechanics.

1. SYNTHETIC — Chrome Silver — "No flourish, no waste."
   Mass-produced android infantry out of the Star Beijing assembly stacks. A Synthetic doesn't have
   a philosophy so much as a spec sheet, and the spec sheet is better than yours. They don't believe
   in cleverness; they believe in tolerances. When a Synthetic unit fails, the factory ships another
   by the following shift change.

2. MEMETIC — Glitch Fuchsia — "An idea doesn't need a body to spread."
   Rogue signal-cults out of the Dalian Block data markets, running their doctrine on hijacked
   ad-servers. A Memetic Commander doesn't field an army so much as release one — a single infected
   thought, copied and re-copied until the battlefield is arguing with itself. Bodies are disposable;
   the idea is what wins.

3. REFINIST — Refined Bronze — "Nothing is waste. Everything is one process away from being useful."
   Alchemist-engineers of Future Odessa, who look at a dying soldier and see feedstock. The Refinist
   creed holds that value is never destroyed, only badly allocated — and that a battlefield is simply
   an inefficient refinery that hasn't met them yet. Slow to start. Nearly unstoppable once the
   crucible is hot.

4. TECHNOPRIEST — Ritual Crimson — "The Machine God does not want your creature. It wants your
   creature's death."
   A techno-religious order out of Mashhad Megacity that reads sacrifice as liturgy. To a Technopriest,
   a creature was never a soldier — it was always a psalm waiting to be sung, and the singing is done
   in blood and static. Every fallen ally makes the next hymn stronger.

5. CYBORG — Acid Lime — "You don't out-think the Redline. You get run over by it."
   Street-augmented brawlers from Dalian Block's underdecks, running on stolen adrenaline and worse
   wiring. No doctrine, no patience, no plan past the next three seconds — just the fastest possible
   route from turn one to your base HP hitting zero.

6. BIOPURE — Deep Jade — "Augment nothing. Grow everything."
   Anti-cybernetic naturalists holding the last green ground in Lahore Republic, in open contempt of
   every other Character on this list. A Biopure Commander doesn't build an army, they cultivate one —
   and cultivation, done right, outlasts anything built.

7. ETHEREAL — Periwinkle — "You cannot counter what isn't fully there yet."
   Phase-shifted energy remnants haunting London Reality's permanent fog — soldiers who are only
   partially committed to existing in this particular timeline. Fragile if you can catch them.
   You will not often catch them.

8. ORGANIZER — Indigo — "One of us is nothing. Forty of us is a supply chain."
   Hive-logistics network out of Star Beijing's warehouse districts. An Organizer Commander does not
   believe in heroes, only throughput — a thousand small, replaceable, well-coordinated things beat one
   large, irreplaceable thing, every time the math is run.

9. SCHEMIST — Shadow Violet — "The winning move was decided three turns before you noticed you were
   playing."
   Deception specialists working the black markets between all six Neo-Cities, loyal to no city because
   loyalty is a lever, and levers belong to whoever's holding them. A Schemist doesn't win the fight you
   think you're in. They win a different one, quietly, underneath it.

================================================================
PART IV — THE EIGHT TRIBES (TEAMS)
================================================================

Tribes (Teams) are cultures, not classes — any Character's deck can carry any Tribe's Colorless cards,
and fields synergy bonuses when enough of one Tribe shares the battlefield (GAME_RULES.md §8). Where a
Character answers "who are you," a Tribe answers "what does your army believe."

1. OVERCHARGERS (Red, #ff2a6d)
   - Philosophy: Strike fast, strike hard
   - Leaders: Neon Striker, Crimson Vanguard
   - Strength: Early game dominance
   - Weakness: Weak late game
   - Cards: Fast, low-cost creatures with Charge

2. FORTRESS GUILD (Green, #05ffa1)
   - Philosophy: Outlast, endure, survive
   - Leaders: Colossus Warden, Living Wall
   - Strength: Survivability, healing
   - Weakness: Slow to start
   - Cards: High defense, structures, healing

3. CONTROL SYNDICATE (Blue, #00d9ff)
   - Philosophy: Deny, disrupt, dominate
   - Leaders: Dominus, Void Weaver
   - Strength: Resource denial, counterplay
   - Weakness: Needs precise timing
   - Cards: Traps, counter-spells, debuffs

4. SYNERGY COLLECTIVE (Purple, #bc13fe)
   - Philosophy: Together we are stronger
   - Leaders: Hive Queen, Echo Mimic
   - Strength: Scaling, combo chains
   - Weakness: Vulnerable to board wipes
   - Cards: Buff stacking, link effects, tokens

5. ECONOMY LORDS (Gold, #ffd700)
   - Philosophy: Invest now, dominate later
   - Leaders: Golden Avatar, Tycoon
   - Strength: Late game power, resource generation
   - Weakness: Weak early game
   - Cards: Power generators, high-cost creatures

6. CHAOS CULT (Black, #ff6b35)
   - Philosophy: Embrace the randomness
   - Leaders: Joker, Chaos Engine
   - Strength: High variance, unpredictable
   - Weakness: Can sabotage yourself
   - Cards: Random effects, coin flips, swaps

7. SYNTH LEGENDS (Cyan, #00f5ff)
   - Philosophy: The old ways are the strongest
   - Leaders: Ancient Guardian, Synth Titan
   - Strength: Single powerful cards, immunity
   - Weakness: Few in number
   - Cards: Ancient, high-stat, immune creatures

8. TITANBORN (Orange, #ff8c00)
   - Philosophy: Size is everything
   - Leaders: World Breaker, Earth Shaker
   - Strength: Massive stats, area effects
   - Weakness: Very slow
   - Cards: Colossal creatures, quake effects

================================================================
PART V — THE CARD SYSTEM (recap)
================================================================

Full mechanical spec: docs/CARDS.md and GAME_RULES.md. Summary:

Every card carries two independent tags — Character (who may own it: one of the 9 above, or Colorless
= anyone) and Team (which Tribe's synergy it feeds).

Seven primary values: Attack (ATK), Defense (DEF), Power (PWR, cost to play), Production (PRD, power
generated per turn), Cost (mana cost to buy), Type (Creature/Spell/Trap/Structure/Action/Gear/
Conspiracy/Role), Team (tribal affiliation).

Secondary values: Speed (attack order), Health (HP), Range (Melee/Ranged/Artillery), Combo ID (legacy
tribal synergy tag), Rarity (Common -> Uncommon -> Rare -> Epic -> Legendary -> Mythic).

================================================================
PART VI — LEXICON
================================================================

BANK PHASE — the card-acquisition phase of a turn; spend Mana to buy from the Metadeck market.
MAIN PHASE — the phase where bought cards are played from hand using Power, and attacks are declared.
RESOLUTION PHASE — end-of-turn phase where played cards resolve in rarity/cost priority and combat lands.
METADECK — the 80-card pool formed by shuffling both players' 40-card decks together at battle start;
   the only source of cards either player will ever hold.
COLORLESS — a card with no Character lock; any of the 9 Characters may run it.
SIGNATURE CARD — one of a Character's 8 class-exclusive cards; only that Character may own it.
TRIBE / TEAM — one of the 8 factional culture tags (Overchargers, Fortress, Control, Synergy, Economy,
   Chaos, Synth Legends, Titanborn) that governs battlefield Ally/Synergy bonuses, independent of Character.
AXIS — one of the five design dials (Cost, Stats, Engine, Choice, Tempo) that fixes a Character's identity.
MANA BURN — HP damage taken for ending the Bank phase with more than 2 unused Mana.
FATIGUE — escalating HP damage taken for drawing from an empty personal deck; three such draws end the game (Exodus).
SUDDEN DEATH — from turn 31 onward, both Commanders take 2 damage per turn until the match resolves.
DANGER EVENT — an escalating hazard (Void Storm, Solar Flare, Gravity Surge, Scavenger Pod, Nebula Bloom)
   that strikes both Commanders every 4 turns starting turn 6.
BATTLE LOG — the full structured, timestamped record of a match's events (buys, plays, attacks, abilities,
   structure changes, win-condition progress), presented as a scrolling, filterable feed during and after battle.

================================================================
PART VII — VICTORY CONDITIONS
================================================================

1. ANNIHILATION: Reduce opponent Base HP to 0
2. DOMINATION: Control 3+ structures for 3 consecutive turns
3. COMBO VICTORY: Field 5 different Tribes simultaneously
4. EXODUS: Deck your opponent (3 forced empty-deck draws, escalating fatigue damage)
5. TIMEOUT: Turn 30 reached with neither side eliminated — highest HP wins, exact tie is a Draw
6. SUDDEN DEATH: From turn 31, both sides take 2 damage/turn until resolution
7. SURRENDER: Forfeit

THE GREAT VAULT AWAITS.
