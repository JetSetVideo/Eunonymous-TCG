World of Synthtopia

The Great Vault

In the year 2187, the 6 Neo-Cities discovered the Great Vault — an ancient repository of infinite power buried beneath the ruins of Old Earth. Each city sent their finest Commanders to claim it, and thus began the Synthopwars.

The 6 Neo-Cities

Dalian Block — Cyber-rain drenched streets where information is the true currency. Traps are hidden in the shadows.

Mashhad Megacity — Desert storm wasteland where only the fast survive. Speed is everything here.

London Reality — Fog-shrouded metropolis where visibility is a luxury. Deception is an art form.

Star Beijing — Neon overload where technology burns bright and hot. Energy courses through every street.

People's Republic of Lahore — Golden hour sanctuary where healing and restoration thrive.

Future Odessa — Gravity-warped zone where reality bends. Nothing is as it seems.

The 9 Characters

Before a Commander builds a deck, they choose what they are. Nine Characters — each with 8 signature cards found nowhere else, a color, and a place on five design axes (Cost, Stats, Engine, Choice, Tempo):

🤖 Synthetic — no flourish, no waste; the best raw stats-per-cost in the game.   🦠 Memetic — an idea doesn't need a body to spread; replicates and infects.   ⚗️ Refinist — nothing is waste, everything is fuel for something bigger.

🕯️ Technopriest — sacrifice as liturgy; every fallen ally sings louder.   🦾 Cyborg — you don't out-think the Redline, you get run over by it.   🌱 Biopure — augment nothing, grow everything.

👻 Ethereal — you cannot counter what isn't fully there yet.   🐝 Organizer — one of us is nothing, forty of us is a supply chain.   ♟️ Schemist — the winning move was decided three turns before you noticed.

The 8 Tribes

Cards also carry a Tribe (Team) — a culture, not a class. Any Character's deck can run any Tribe's cards; fielding enough of one Tribe together grants Ally/Synergy bonuses. From the aggressive Overchargers to the unpredictable Chaos Cult, every battle is a clash of ideologies as much as armies — layered on top of the Character you chose to fight as.

The Cards

Each card represents a unit, spell, trap, or structure from the Synthtopia universe. They carry the power of the 7 values: Attack, Defense, Power, Production, Cost, Type, and Team. The rarest cards — the Mythics — are said to contain fragments of the Vault itself.

Victory Conditions

Win by reducing your opponent's Base HP to 0, controlling their structures for 3 turns, or activating the legendary 5-card Combo Win. The Great Vault rewards only the most cunning Commanders.

How to Play

Quick Start

1. Build a Deck — Select 40 cards (min 20 creatures). Each card has a Power cost.

2. Choose Battleground — Pick a Neo-City with special rules.

3. Battle! — Draw cards, play creatures, attack the enemy Base.

Card Values

⚔️ Attack — Damage dealt to enemies

🛡️ Defense — Damage absorbed before HP loss

⚡ Power — Cost to play the card (you gain Power each turn)

🏭 Production — Power generated per turn while on field

📈 Speed — Attack order (higher goes first)

🎯 Range — Melee (1), Ranged (2), Artillery (3)

Turn Phases

Draw: Draw 1 card from your deck.

Power: Gain base Power (equal to turn number, max 12).

Main: Play creatures, spells, traps, structures. Costs Power.

Battle: Creatures attack. Higher Speed attacks first.

End: Excess cards discarded. Turn passes to opponent.

Victory Conditions

1. Annihilation: Reduce enemy Base HP to 0.

2. Domination: Control 3 enemy structures for 3 turns.

3. Exodus: Enemy runs out of cards (fatigue damage).

Deck Strategies

Overcharge (Red): Fast, aggressive. Rush with low-cost creatures.

Fortress (Green): Defensive, healing. Build walls and outlast.

Control (Blue): Disruptive. Deny resources, counter spells.

Synergy (Purple): Buff stacking. Link creatures for combos.

Economy (Gold): Late game. Invest Power for massive returns.

Chaos (Black): Random. High variance, unpredictable outcomes.

Combo System

Cards with the same Combo ID activate synergies when multiple are on the field:

• Twin Strike (Rush): 2+ Rush cards = +2 ATK each.

• Link Sync (Link): 2+ Link cards = +1/+1 each.

• Fortress Wall (Wall): 2+ Wall cards = +2 DEF each.

• Gold Rush (Gold): 3+ Gold cards = +2 Power generation.

Battleground Effects

Each Neo-City changes the rules. Choose your battlefield wisely!

• Dalian Block: Traps hidden. Spells cost +1.

• Mashhad: Slow creatures cannot attack first turn.

• London: Base row hidden until attacked.

• Star Beijing: Spells cost -1. Creatures lose 1 HP/turn.

• Lahore: Healing doubled. Structures regenerate.

• Odessa: Max hand size = 7. Excess draws discarded.
